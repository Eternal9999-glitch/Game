How to GooBall

The idea

Score the ball into the enemy net. There is no kick button — everything is physics. Bump the ball, dash into it, or park on top of it for 3 seconds to absorb it, then dash to launch it like a cannonball. Getting dashed while carrying knocks it loose.

Dash & damage

Hold dash to charge, release to fly. A fully-charged dash that lands on the enemy deals damage. Lose all HP → you're out for 5s and respawn at your goal. Cards hurt too. Highest score after 5 minutes wins; ties go to golden goal.

Cards & points

Before the match, pick 6 of 15 cards. In-match you gain +1⭐ every 5s (start with 3, cap 10); cards cost ⭐ to play. Explode, saw, tornado-steal the ball, wall off your net… points reset to 3 after every goal.

Countries

You don't play as a real nation — you found your own. Name it, design the flag, make it feared.
