TURF WARS

multiplayer pixel territory party game · one HTML file
Goal: own the most turf when the 5-minute timer ends.
Economy: +1 coin every second — spend coins on units.
Move: click one of your units, then drag a path — it follows your line and paints every tile it crosses. Tap ground to send it straight there. Tap the unit again to halt it.
Battle: when enemy units touch, the lower-power unit is destroyed and the winner's owner earns +1 gold. Units also defend nearby tiles from being painted.
Units: 1 Dart (fast, fragile) · 2 Guard (all-rounder) · 3 Titan (slow, mighty)
